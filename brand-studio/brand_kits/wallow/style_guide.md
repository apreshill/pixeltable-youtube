# wallow brand guidelines

Version 1.0. For partners, agencies, and the in-house studio.

## Who we are

wallow makes bath soaks and candles for slow evenings. A long-soak bath oil, a mineral salt soak, and a candle for the edge of the tub. To wallow is to stay in a little longer, on purpose. Our world is the hour after the day is done: the bath running, a candle lit, nowhere left to be.

## Voice

- Warm, unhurried, and a little indulgent. We give permission to linger.
- We talk about evenings, warmth, and staying in, never about stress or escape.
- Short lines. A headline can be three words.
- Headlines are lowercase, with no full stop.
- We never say "self-care", "unwind", "detox", or "treat yourself".

Examples: "stay in a little longer" "the bath is the plan" "one more chapter, one more candle"

## Palette

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Tallow | #F2E8D5 | Backgrounds, labels |
| Tobacco | #4A2E1F | Type, logo on light grounds |
| Oxblood | #6E1F24 | Caps, lids, one accent per layout |
| Amber | #C98A3B | Glass, candlelight, warm highlights |
| Smoke | #CFC6BA | Soft fills, linens |

Warm creams and browns carry every layout. Oxblood is an accent, never a background.

## Typography

- Headlines: Fraunces, semibold, lowercase, set close.
- Body and buttons: Work Sans Regular. Buttons are lowercase.
- The wordmark is wallow in Fraunces Black with the soft axis turned all the way up, all lowercase, set close together. Its letters are plump and round-cornered, like something pressed into warm wax. Never capitalize it. Never redraw it. Use the logo file.

## Logo

- Use the Tobacco logo on light grounds and the Tallow logo on dark grounds.
- Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the letter a.

## Photography

- Warm, low evening light: the last of the daylight, lamplight, candlelight.
- Water and warmth: a deep bath, soft steam, the glow of amber glass.
- No people. The ritual is told by what is left out: a folded towel, a lit candle, a book face down.
- Surfaces: white enamel, warm wood, linen, unglazed ceramic, amber glass.
- Props, when used: a candle, a folded linen towel, a jug of garden flowers. Never more than two.
- Never: people or any part of a person, cold or blue light, harsh flash, glitter, clutter, plastic.

## Packaging

- Bath oil: a round amber glass bottle with an Oxblood cap and a cream paper label.
- Candle: a matte cream ceramic vessel with an Oxblood ceramic lid.
