# ORIEL brand guidelines

Version 3.2. For partners, agencies, and the in-house studio.

## Who we are

ORIEL makes complexion and skin care for people who want their skin to look like skin. Skin tints, cheek and lip balms, and a daily SPF serum. Everything is built around one idea: skin, in better light. An oriel is a bay window that lets light in from three sides. That light is our whole look.

## Voice

- Warm, calm, and exact. We talk like a friend who works in a lab.
- Short sentences. One idea at a time.
- We describe what the product does on skin, never what it hides.
- We never say "flawless", "perfect", "anti-aging", "fix", or "miracle".
- Headlines are sentence case. They often end with a full stop.
- One or two words in a headline may be set in the italic serif for emphasis, never more.

Examples: "Skin, in *better light*." "Sheer tint. Real skin." "*Morning* light, all day."

## Palette

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Linen | #F4EDE3 | Backgrounds, packaging base |
| Espresso | #33241D | Type, logo on light grounds |
| Apricot | #E9A47F | Accent, caps, highlights |
| Terracotta | #A9553A | Buttons and calls to action |
| Honey | #F3D7AE | Soft fills, light on surfaces |

Warm neutrals carry every layout. Apricot is an accent, never a background. No pure white, no pure black, no cool greys, no blue.

## Typography

- Headlines: Jost Medium, tight letter spacing.
- Emphasis: Cormorant Garamond Medium Italic, for one or two words in a headline.
- Body and buttons: Jost Regular. Buttons are uppercase with wide letter spacing.
- The wordmark is ORIEL in Jost Light, uppercase, with very wide letter spacing. Never redraw it. Use the logo file.

## Logo

- Use the Espresso logo on light grounds and the Linen logo on dark or busy grounds.
- Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the letter O.
- Never stretch, outline, add effects to, or recolor the logo outside the palette.

## Photography

- Natural, late-morning daylight through a window. Warm, soft, directional.
- Window light falls across the scene: soft shadows of panes and sheer curtains.
- No people. We never show faces, hands, or bodies. Skin is shown as texture instead: a sheer swatch of tint on warm stone, a balm pressed open in its compact.
- Surfaces: travertine, limestone, raw linen, unglazed ceramic, warm plaster walls.
- Props, when used: a sprig of dried grass, a ceramic dish, a folded linen napkin. Never more than two.
- Colors in the frame stay inside the palette: creams, sand, apricot, terracotta, warm brown.
- Composition: calm, lots of negative space, one clear subject.
- Never: people or any part of a person, studio strobes, hard flash, neon, glitter, cool or blue light, busy patterns, marble with grey veins.

## Packaging

- Skin tint: a frosted glass dropper bottle with an apricot dropper bulb and a short Espresso ORIEL wordmark printed on the front.
- Balms: a squat matte Linen compact with the ORIEL wordmark debossed on the lid.
- Serum: a frosted glass pump bottle with a Terracotta pump.
