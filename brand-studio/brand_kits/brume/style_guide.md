# brume brand guidelines

Version 2.0. For partners, agencies, and the in-house studio.

## Who we are

brume makes hair care for slow mornings. A pre-wash scalp oil, a weightless hair mist, a silk-protein conditioner, and the tools to use them: a wide-tooth comb and silk scrunchies. Brume is French for mist. Our world is the bathroom at 7am: steam on the mirror, water running, nowhere to be yet.

## Voice

- Quiet, sensory, and a little poetic. We slow the reader down.
- We talk about rituals and time, never about problems or damage.
- Short lines. Lots of air. A headline can be a single phrase.
- We never say "repair", "damage", "frizz control", "hack", or "must-have".
- Headlines are sentence case, with no full stop.
- One or two words in a headline may be set in italic for emphasis, never more.

Examples: "Slow hair, *soft* mornings" "Let the steam *do the work*" "A ritual, not a routine"

## Palette

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Fog | #E8EAE5 | Backgrounds, packaging base |
| Slate | #1E2A2F | Type, logo on light grounds |
| Sage | #8DA290 | Accent, labels, bottle tint |
| Moss | #4E5F50 | Buttons and calls to action |
| Butter | #F0E5B5 | Warm highlight, used sparingly |

Cool, soft greens and greys carry every layout. Butter is the only warm color and covers no more than a tenth of a layout. No pure black, no bright white, no orange, no gold.

## Typography

- Headlines: Instrument Serif Regular, large, sentence case, with normal letter spacing.
- Emphasis: Instrument Serif Italic, for one or two words in a headline.
- Body and buttons: DM Mono Regular, small. Buttons are uppercase with wide letter spacing. The typewriter voice is the counterpoint to the soft serif.
- The wordmark is brume in DM Mono Medium, all lowercase, spaced a little open, like a typewritten label on a pharmacy bottle. It is built on a grid, so it can be printed, woven, stenciled, or laid in tile. Never capitalize it, even at the start of a sentence. Never redraw it. Use the logo file.

## Logo

- Use the Slate logo on light grounds and the Fog logo on dark or busy grounds.
- Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the letter b.
- Never stretch, outline, add effects to, or recolor the logo outside the palette.

## Photography

- Soft, diffused morning light, slightly cool, filtered through steam or frosted glass.
- Water and steam everywhere: droplets on glass, condensation on tile, a haze in the air.
- No people. We never show faces, hands, hair on a head, or bodies. The ritual is told by what it leaves behind: a damp comb, a scrunchie on the basin edge, a towel still warm from the shower.
- Surfaces: white zellige tile, brushed nickel, frosted glass, porcelain, damp stone.
- Props, when used: a folded waffle towel, a wide-tooth comb, a single eucalyptus stem. Never more than two.
- Colors in the frame stay inside the palette: fog, sage, moss, slate, with a small touch of butter.
- Composition: close, intimate, shallow depth of field, lots of soft haze.
- Never: people or any part of a person, hard sunlight, warm orange light, gold, glitter, salon chairs, blow-dryers, busy backgrounds.

## Packaging

- Hair oil: a frosted sage-tinted glass bottle with a brushed nickel dropper and the brume wordmark printed in Slate.
- Hair mist: a matte Fog-colored aluminum spray bottle with a brushed nickel cap and the brume wordmark in Slate.
- Conditioner: a soft-touch Fog tube with a Moss cap.
